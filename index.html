        Milestones

        What each level unlocks and what to do about it

          Forever-specific items come from Blizzard's Priest deep dive and beta notes as collected on the Four Roads Priest page; spell levels without a tag are Classic and may have moved. Check the trainer.

          Legacy

          Account-wide points, 16 spendable per character at launch

          Sixty-five challenges, one point each. Twenty-seven are class milestones at 25, 45 and 60, so your four roads are a point engine: four characters at 25 is four points. Tradeskill points come at 150, 225 and 300 in non-gathering primaries, which is why repeating profession pairs across alts costs you points later. Dungeon points come from the final boss of each tier.

          Earn first

          Kooala 25 (1). Tailoring 150 (1). Tier-one final boss (1). Then each alt at 25.

          The roster as an engine

          Eight of nine classes means the class milestones alone can pay 24 points over time. The six crafting trades (Tailoring, Enchanting, Leatherworking, Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering) pay 18 more at 150, 225 and 300, but only once each, so a spread beats a repeat: Kooala keeps Tailoring and Enchanting, Quookka Leatherworking, and Walaroo, Woombat, Mars, Bandicoot and Oopossum could take the other four between them with gathering to feed them. A week-two decision, not a launch-week one.

          Spend candidates

          Thrill of Adventure (health and mana after kills; a priest's best friend). Well Rested (parked alts). Talented (points up to five levels early). Field Guide (shorter camping cooldown). Read the live tree before the first point; no spend order is promised here.

          Opinion, yours to overrule

          Wand, solved. Deepblaze, the blue wand from the Hall of Thanes quest Old Ironforge Incursion (22 to 41 Fire damage at 1.70 speed, 18.5 damage per second), replaced the practice wand at 14, and Wand Specialization multiplies it. Nothing Enchanting can make comes close until the 40s, so wand upgrades from here are quest and dungeon drops; the Dwarven Tome off-hand from the same dungeon (+2 Spirit, +4 spell damage and healing) is the other keeper.

          The 30-to-40 decision. Dual spec arrives at 40, so one tree has to do both jobs only between 30 and 40. Dungeon healing before 40 runs on trained spells (Shield, Renew, Heal, Flash Heal at 20, Prayer of Healing at 30); talents only move the margins. The rule: if most evenings in that stretch are dungeons, respec at 30 to the Discipline bridge (Wand Specialization 2, Twin Disciplines 5, Mental Strength 5, Mental Agility 3, Improved Shield 3, Meditation 2, Penance) and buy Shadow back at 40 as the second spec. If most evenings are solo with the odd run, stay Shadow to 40: Spirit Tap also fires when a mob carrying your Vampiric Embrace dies (from 27), so packs refill you even when the tank gets the kill, and Shadowform at 40 is the biggest solo spike the class has.

          At 60. Two specs, no compromise: Shadow for solo, and a Holy-heavy healing tree for groups (about 19/32/0, Discipline for Meditation, Inner Focus and Mental Strength). Every pre-raid ranking projects Holy Priest as the top raid healer; raids open 9 December and will say whether they were right. Prayer of Mending is Holy's 35-point capstone (earliest 44, behind Spirit of Redemption); a 15/36/0 keeps Meditation and Inner Focus and takes it, a fair trade for dungeon healing, and it is the reason Holy tops the raid lists.

          Respec costs climb within a week and reset weekly (TechRadar via Wowhead, Medium). Budget one deliberate respec around 25 to 30, when Penance and Legacy are both in reach. Dual spec unlocks at 40 at the class trainer (Blizzard); its price has not been posted. That makes 30 to 40 the only stretch where one tree has to do both jobs. Ladder checked against the live tree (Talents Forever, game build 70755) and the Kargoz, Mobalytics and Method levelling guides, 6 November.

          Leatrix Plus Week two

          One convenience module at a time, only for something you missed.

          Why so few: Forever hides health, auras and cooldowns from addons in combat and gives them no combat log, so Classic-era addons break or go blank.           Settings worth touching

          Five, then play

            The thumb grid

            Naga V3 Pro, no software needed

            Razer's own support page says it plainly (updated 14 Oct 2026): the V3 Pro is not supported in Synapse for Mac, and it is plug-and-play without software. Out of the box the twelve thumb buttons send the number row, 1 to 9 then 0, minus and equals, which is exactly what Action Bar 1 is bound to. The thumb grid is Action Bar 1. The keyboard's number row duplicates it, which is the fallback if the mouse dies. The extras on this model (tilt wheel, DPI edge buttons, ring-finger button, the AI Prompt button) send nothing useful until Synapse assigns them, and Synapse has to run on Windows to do it. The mouse keeps the result in on-board memory (Razer: key bindings and DPI stages persist without Synapse; up to five profiles on this model), so one pass on a Windows machine is enough. Do it for the ring finger and the tilt wheel, not for the grid.

            Modifiers are WoW's layers and Caps is the leader key. The same thumb button does the same job in every layer position: 5 is always the shield, Caps+5 shields you, Shift+5 is Fortitude.

            One-time setup, in game

              The Windows pass, once

              Fifteen minutes on a Windows machine with Synapse 4. After it, the mouse carries the profile and the Mac never needs Razer software.

                The screen mirrors the thumb

                Edit Mode, 3 icons by 4 rows

                The bars on screen have three jobs: show cooldowns, show procs and range, and teach the thumb where things are. All three work better when the block on screen has the shape of the block under your thumb. Forever's Edit Mode can draw a 12-icon bar as 4 rows, so Action Bar 1 becomes a 3 by 4 block and Action Bar 2 a second one beside it. Put both under the character, next to the party frames, where a healer's eyes already live. Nothing else on screen needs to move.

                    From Scribe

                    /pouch in the game, Cmd+C, paste here

                    Pouch Scribe is the addon. Its line adds a Kooala entry for that session (or updates the one you pasted earlier), sets any alt's level, and fills the profession, talent and gold bars. What broke and what was fun stay yours: add them on the entry.

                    No clipboard needed: the game writes every session to _forever_/WTF/Account/DKORMUSHOFF/SavedVariables/PouchScribe.lua at logout or /reload. Pick it, drop it on the box, or (Chrome on the Mac, when this page is open as its own tab) let the browser remember it so one button re-reads it. In the picker, Cmd+Shift+G takes the path.
